Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

1. Information We Collect

At Boomin Business, we collect information to provide better services to our customers purchasing computer hardware, processors, graphics cards, and related technology products.

Personal Information

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you contact us or make purchases
  • Shipping and billing addresses for product deliveries
  • Payment information (processed securely through our payment partners)
  • Product preferences and purchase history

Technical Information

  • Device specifications when requesting compatibility advice
  • Browser type and version for website optimization
  • IP address and location data for shipping calculations

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Processing and fulfilling your hardware orders
  • Providing technical support for processors, graphics cards, and other components
  • Sending product updates and compatibility notifications
  • Improving our product recommendations based on your system requirements
  • Communicating about warranty services and product recalls
  • Analyzing website performance and user experience

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With shipping partners to deliver your hardware products
  • With payment processors to complete transactions securely
  • With manufacturers for warranty claims on processors, graphics cards, and other components
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • With your explicit consent for specific purposes

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure storage of customer and order information
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal data by authorized personnel only
  • Secure disposal of hardware containing customer data

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Essential cookies for website functionality and shopping cart
  • Analytics cookies to understand product preferences
  • Marketing cookies for personalized hardware recommendations
  • You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access to your personal data and purchase history
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of your personal data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Data portability for your order and product information

7. International Transfers

As we serve customers globally for computer hardware products, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of significant changes through email or website notifications.

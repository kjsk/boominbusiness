About BoominBusiness

Your trusted partner in computer parts import and export

Our Story

Founded in 2014, BoominBusiness emerged from a simple vision: to bridge the gap between computer hardware manufacturers and distributors worldwide. What started as a small operation has grown into a trusted international partner serving clients across 50+ countries.

Our founders, with over 20 years of combined experience in the technology sector, recognized the need for a reliable, efficient, and transparent import-export service specifically tailored to the computer parts industry. Today, we continue to uphold those founding principles while embracing innovation and technological advancement.
